# Rubrics (dimensions are scored 1-5; 5 = excellent, 3 = acceptable, 1 = broken)

Scores: 1 broken - 2 clear problems - 3 acceptable - 4 good - 5 excellent. A dimension at 3 needs no defect to be listed.

## video
- **content_accuracy**: facts, names, numbers are right and match the script/source.
- **story_flow**: hook in the first seconds, clear sequence, a satisfying ending; no confusing jumps.
- **picture_logic**: objects and characters behave plausibly; nothing floats, clips, duplicates or has wrong parts; one consistent visual style.
- **text_and_captions**: legible on a phone, inside safe areas, spelled right, on the word being said, not colliding with objects.
- **timing_sync**: cuts and captions land on the words they illustrate; pacing neither rushed nor dragging.
- **audio**: voice clear and audible over music, no clipping, no long silences, no mispronounced words.
- **polish**: no black or frozen frames, no flicker or artifacts, clean start and end.

## image
- **purpose_fit**: does what the request needs, right subject and message.
- **composition**: clear focal point, balance, nothing cut off by accident.
- **legibility**: any text readable at the target size with enough contrast.
- **consistency**: style, colors, perspective and anatomy/objects consistent.
- **technical**: resolution, aspect ratio, artifacts, compression.

## text
- **accuracy**: every fact, figure, quote and name checked against a source.
- **clarity**: main point first, plain sentences, no ambiguity.
- **audience_fit**: right level, tone and length for the reader.
- **structure**: logical order, scannable where useful.
- **originality_and_rights**: own wording, no copied passages.

## code
- **correctness**: runs on real input and gives the right output (you ran it).
- **robustness**: edge cases and errors handled.
- **simplicity**: smallest design that works; no needless abstraction.
- **readability**: clear names, comments on why.
- **safety**: no secrets, no destructive surprises, inputs validated.

## generic
- **meets_request**, **correctness**, **completeness**, **quality**, **risk**.

## Defect severity
- **3 = blocker**: breaks the purpose, factual error, unreadable, broken or silent audio, crash, unsafe.
- **2 = serious**: noticeable to most viewers or readers, hurts credibility or experience.
- **1 = minor**: polish, taste, small inconsistency.

## Example project-specific video defects (faceless-shorts style; adapt to your skill)
Body parts or clothing inconsistent (for example see-through or mismatched sleeves), props floating or in the wrong hand,
two moving things crossing each other or a label, caption zone collisions, more than the allowed words in a caption, wrong
highlighted word, misspelling, camera motion that hides the subject, abrupt cuts mid-sentence.

## How to look (vision)
Look at many frames, not three. Check the first frame, the last frame, every cut, and moments where things move. Zoom into
text and into hands, faces, edges. Compare consecutive frames for sudden changes. State exactly which frames or timestamps you
viewed so the user can trust the review.
